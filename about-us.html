About UrbanNest

A property team working across Johannesburg, Pretoria, Cape Town and Durban, for tenants and buyers alike.

Property, handled properly

UrbanNest helps people Find a home to rent and a index to buy. We list apartments, studios, townhouses and family houses in four South African cities, and we handle developments as they come to market.

Every property on this website is visited in person before it goes up. We check the rooms, the finishes, the light and the neighbours, so that what you read here matches what you see when you arrive.

We work for both sides of the market. If you are renting, we explain the deposit, the lease and the notice period before you sign anything. If you are buying, we walk you through the offer, the bond process and the transfer timeline. Either way, you deal with one person from your first enquiry to your keys.

What We Stand For

Three promises we keep on every listing, whether it is a studio or a family house.

Visited in person

No listing goes live on photographs alone. A member of our team has stood in the room and checked it.

One point of contact

The person who answers your enquiry is the person who books your viewing and follows up afterwards.

Costs stated upfront

Rent, deposit, levies and purchase price are on the listing, not saved for the phone call.

How It Works

Four steps from browsing to keys, whether you are renting or buying.

  1. 1

    Search the listings

    Filter by city, suburb, property type, price, bedrooms, bathrooms and floor size to narrow the list down.

  2. 2

    Enquire about a property

    Send an enquiry from the listing or the enquiry page. We confirm availability and the full cost breakdown.

  3. 3

    Book a viewing

    Choose a date that suits you and we confirm a time with the current owner or tenant.

  4. 4

    Sign and move in

    We explain every clause before you sign the lease or the offer to purchase, then hand over the keys.
