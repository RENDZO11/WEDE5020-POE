What to Check Before You Sign a Lease

2 October 2026 · by the UrbanNest team

A lease is a contract, and the details that cause trouble later are usually the ones nobody read. Before you sign, work through the deposit, the escalation, the notice period and the maintenance split, and ask for anything unclear to be put in writing.

Start with the deposit. Confirm how much it is, whether interest is paid on it, and the conditions under which it is refunded. Then check the escalation clause, so you know what your rent becomes in the second year rather than being surprised by it.

Finally, walk the property with the lease in hand. Note every existing crack, chip and stain on the incoming inspection list, photograph the meter readings, and keep a signed copy. That list is what protects your deposit when you move out.
